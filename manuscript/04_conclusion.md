# Conclusion

**{{BOND_IDENTITY}}** demonstrates how a seemingly non-violent lever — control
of water and scarce commodities — produces coercive power, and how deterministic
algorithms make that power measurable. The package ships a pure, infrastructure-free
domain core (hydrology, water cartel, commodity-cartel graph) both for direct
use and as the substrate of a `bond_api` mission provider, so the BOND
orchestrator can discover and drive *Quantum of Solace* exactly like any other
film.

The headline result is that leverage is systemic: a cartel with minority dam
ownership and a lead in two high-value commodities commands disproportionate
influence over downstream demand and market concentration. The methods — mass
balance, Dijkstra, Edmonds–Karp, reservoir simulation, and the
Herfindahl–Hirschman index — are all standard, all real, and all reproduced
deterministically. No metric in this manuscript is hand-authored.
